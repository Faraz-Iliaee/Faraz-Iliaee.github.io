Faraz Iliaee

Hello and thanks for stopping by!

My name is Faraz Iliaee. I recently completed my MSc in Chemistry at Concordia University in Montreal, where I worked under the supervision of Dr. Xianming Zhang. During my graduate studies, I investigated the occurrence, transformation, and fate of organic contaminants using high-resolution mass spectrometry and computational tools.

My work combines advanced analytical instrumentation, chromatographic techniques, method development, and data analysis to investigate complex chemical systems across environmental and biological matrices. Using targeted and non-targeted workflows alongside statistical and computational approaches in R and Python, I aim to transform complex chemical datasets into meaningful insights that support contaminant prioritization, risk assessment, and scientific decision-making.

When I'm not in the lab, you'll probably find me outdoors. I enjoy hiking, cycling, surfing, and exploring new places whenever I have the opportunity. Whether it's a mountain trail, a long bike ride, or a day near the water, I appreciate activities that keep me connected to nature. Spending time outdoors has given me a deeper appreciation for the environment and continues to inspire my interest in understanding and protecting the natural systems that support our everyday lives.
