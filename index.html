Faraz Iliaee

Hello and thanks for stopping by!

My name is Faraz Iliaee. I recently completed my MSc in Chemistry at Concordia University in Montreal, where I worked under the supervision of Dr. Xianming Zhang. During my graduate studies, I investigated the occurrence, transformation, and environmental behavior of organic contaminants using high-resolution mass spectrometry and computational analysis. I enjoy combining analytical chemistry, environmental science, and data-driven approaches to better understand how contaminants move through our environment and impact ecosystems and human health.

Right now, I particularly enjoy working on the following topics:

  • Environmental sources, processes, and impacts of organic contaminants
  • Human exposure to organic contaminants in everyday environments
  • Long-range transport and long-term fate of persistent organic pollutants
  • Environmental monitoring and chemical characterization using high-resolution mass spectrometry
  • Environmental data analysis and computational modeling
  • Models that simulate environmental processes, exposures, and contaminant impacts
